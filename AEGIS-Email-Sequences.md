Dear [First Name],

**1. A structured risk assessment model.** Not just "we visit high-risk branches more often" but a documented, multi-parameter scoring system that produces transparent, traceable branch-level risk scores. The scoring methodology must connect directly to the audit plan.

# GLOBAL RULES

## Exit Conditions

- **Launch → Onboarding:** Contact books a demo, starts a pilot, or replies with interest → exit Launch, enter Nurture or Onboarding as appropriate
- **Nurture → Onboarding:** Contact starts a sandbox pilot → exit Nurture, enter Onboarding
- **Onboarding → Manual:** Contact starts a real-data pilot → exit Onboarding, handle via personal relationship
- **Any → Pause:** Contact replies "not interested" or "check back later" → exit all sequences, add to "Warm, Not Ready" list, re-enter in 90 days

## Suppression Rules

- Never send more than 2 emails per week to any contact
- Do not send if contact has unsubscribed
- Do not send if contact has an active support conversation
- Do not send Launch emails to contacts already in Nurture or Onboarding

## Re-Entry Rules

- Contacts who complete Launch without converting → re-enter Nurture in 90 days with a fresh angle
- Contacts who complete a sandbox pilot without converting to paid → quarterly check-in (manual, not automated)

# A/B TEST RECOMMENDATIONS

**Test 1: Subject Line Style**

- Version A: Problem-focused ("Your audit team deserves better than Excel")
- Version B: Feature-focused ("568 items. 18 modules. Zero setup.")
- Split: 50/50 on Launch Email 1
- Measure: Open rate after 48 hours

**Test 2: CTA Phrasing**

- Version A: "Start your free sandbox pilot →"
- Version B: "See AEGIS in action — no commitment →"
- Split: 50/50 on Nurture Email 3
- Measure: Click-through rate

**Test 3: Email Length**

- Version A: Full email as drafted (150-200 words)
- Version B: Shortened to 80-100 words with same CTA
- Split: 50/50 on Launch Email 4
- Measure: Reply rate
