**[Screen: Switch to HIA role → RAM (Risk Assessment) page]**

"The Head of Internal Audit starts with risk assessment. AEGIS scores every branch across 19 parameters — credit risk, operational risk, compliance, and more. These scores are color-coded and ranked automatically."

**[Click into a branch RAM score, show the parameter breakdown]**

"From these scores, AEGIS generates a risk-ranked annual audit plan. High-risk branches get audited more frequently. The plan updates when risk profiles change."

**[Show audit plan view with branch calendar]**

## Production Notes

**Recording tips:**

- Use a clean demo tenant with realistic sample data (3-5 branches, mix of findings)
- Record at 1920x1080 resolution
- Keep mouse movements deliberate and smooth — pause on key screens for 2-3 seconds
- Use Loom for easy recording + sharing, or OBS for more control

**Voice tips:**

- Conversational, confident tone — not salesy
- Speak at a measured pace (150 words/minute)
- Pause briefly between sections to let the screen speak

**Editing tips:**

- Add subtle zoom-ins on key dashboard elements (health score, risk scores, finding timeline)
- Include a 2-second AEGIS title card at the start and end
- Add light background music (royalty-free, instrumental, low volume)
- Total spoken words: ~430 (fits within 3 minutes at natural pace)

**Where to publish:**

- YouTube (unlisted or public — link in emails and LinkedIn posts)
- Embed on aegis.nexlyadvisory.com landing page
- Share directly via WhatsApp to warm leads
