### Post 2 — Educational (RBI Risk Based Internal Audit)

> **RBI's Risk Based Internal Audit (RBIA) framework isn't optional anymore.**
>
> If your UCB hasn't adopted RBIA, here's what the framework requires:
>
> 1. Risk Assessment Model (RAM) — score every branch across 19 parameters
> 2. Risk-based audit plan — prioritize high-risk branches for more frequent audits
> 3. Structured examination — 568 items across cash, loans, SMA/NPA, investments, and more
> 4. Compliance lifecycle — findings → branch response → reviewing officer → ACB
> 5. Board reporting — quarterly summaries with risk trends and resolution rates
>
> Most UCBs know the "what." The challenge is the "how" — especially with a small audit team covering 10+ branches.
>
> That's exactly the problem we built AEGIS to solve.
>
