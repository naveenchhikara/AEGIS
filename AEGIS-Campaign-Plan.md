# AEGIS Go-to-Market Campaign Plan

**Campaign:** "Audit Transformed" — UCB Pilot Acquisition
**Duration:** 8 weeks (March–April 2026)
**Budget:** Lean / Bootstrap (~₹50K total)
**Goal:** 3–5 UCBs in free sandbox pilot by Week 8

---

## Objective

Generate 3–5 qualified pilot signups from Tier III/IV Urban Cooperative Banks within 8 weeks, using a lean outreach strategy focused on direct engagement with CEOs, HIAs, and Board members.

SMART goal: "Secure 3 signed Pilot A agreements with UCBs by April 30, 2026, with zero paid media spend, using direct outreach, LinkedIn organic, and referral-based channels."

---

## Target Audience

### Primary: UCB Chief Executive Officers (CEOs / MDs)

Profile: "A CEO or Managing Director of a Tier III/IV Urban Cooperative Bank with 5–50 branches, who is under RBI scrutiny for audit compliance, frustrated with paper-based or Excel-driven internal audit processes, and looking for an affordable, ready-to-deploy technology solution. They discover tools through industry associations (NAFCUB, state federations), peer recommendations, and RBI circulars. They care most about regulatory compliance, cost, and ease of adoption."

Pain points: RBI inspection anxiety, manual audit backlogs, regulatory compliance pressure, limited IT budgets, no in-house tech team.

### Secondary: Head of Internal Audit (HIA) / Internal Audit Heads

Profile: "An HIA or Audit Head at a UCB responsible for managing 5–20 audits per year across branches, who spends most of their time on coordination, follow-ups, and manual report compilation. They need a system that automates the audit lifecycle from planning through reporting, supports their team of 2–5 auditors, and generates RBI-ready documentation."

### Tertiary: Board Members / Audit Committee Chairs

Profile: "A Board member or ACB Chair who receives quarterly audit reports but has no real-time visibility into audit status, compliance gaps, or risk scores. They want a dashboard that gives them oversight without requiring them to dig through paper files."
